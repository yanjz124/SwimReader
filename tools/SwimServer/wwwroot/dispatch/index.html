A timetable of real flights from recent SWIM/SFDPS traffic: when each one departed (UTC), who flew it, the type and tail, and the gates: departure gate from TDLS, and an arrival gate marked * inferred from where the same airframe next departed (no feed publishes arrival gates). Gate out/in times come from TFMS airline data. Use the AIRLINE and TYPE chips to narrow the list — pick several in a row to include them all, or one in each row to require both. Click a flight for its filed route and how many others filed the same routing. Open in SimBrief pre-fills the route, cruise, tail (reg, SELCAL, Mode-S) and the SimBrief variant you choose; SimBrief can then prefile to VATSIM. Prefile on VATSIM skips SimBrief and opens VATSIM's prefile page with a complete ICAO plan already loaded. A few flights have no recorded departure time (shown as —); they're placed by their ETA. Routes are shown as filed — tidy them in SimBrief if a fix has aged out of your nav data.
