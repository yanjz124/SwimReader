ROUTE FINDER

real filed routes → SimBrief / VATSIM
Any combination works — a city pair, an airline, an aircraft type, or all three. 3-letter airports (ORD) are fine.
Routes are real filed flight plans from recent SWIM/SFDPS traffic. BY ROUTE groups flights that filed the same routing, most-flown first — the quickest way to find a realistic route. Open in SimBrief pre-fills origin, destination, aircraft, route, cruise and registration; just hit Generate, and SimBrief can prefile straight to VATSIM. Copy plan gives an ICAO-style block for manual prefiling, Copy route just the route string. Gate/runway come from TDLS when the flight was seen recently. Routes are shown as filed — tidy them in SimBrief if a fix has aged out of your nav data.
