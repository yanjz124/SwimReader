ROUTE FINDER

real SWIM flights → SimBrief / VATSIM
Search by any combination of route, airline, or aircraft type.
Data is real filed flight plans pulled from SWIM/SFDPS history (the last few days of traffic). Each result opens in SimBrief with the origin, destination, aircraft, route, cruise, and registration pre-filled — just hit Generate. SimBrief can then prefile straight to VATSIM. Copy plan gives you an ICAO-style block for manual VATSIM prefiling. Gate/runway come from live TDLS when the flight was seen recently. Routes are shown as filed; tidy them in SimBrief if a fix has aged out of the nav data.
