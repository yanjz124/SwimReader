FLOW
An FCA is a Flow Constrained Area — a real constraint. An FEA is a Flow Evaluation Area, a traffic manager watching a volume with nothing restricted. TFMS publishes both in the same shape and there are far more FEAs, so they are separate switches: drawing them alike would paint the country with boxes that mean nothing is wrong. Areas outside their own published window are not drawn — many are defined days ahead.
Arrival metering from TBFM and surface/departure constraint from TFDM. They are not added together — one is airborne holding, the other is the field. Ring size is the metering hold; amber means TFDM has something in force.
